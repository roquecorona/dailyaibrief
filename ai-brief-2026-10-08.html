Top Story

Assistants  ·  Oct 7, 2026

GPT-6 comes to every ChatGPT tier — and answers now arrive as interactive "Intelligent UI"

OpenAI began rolling GPT-6 into ChatGPT on Wednesday: GPT-6 Sol for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users, and the smaller GPT-6 Luna for Free and Go users starting today. The headline feature is "Intelligent UI" — instead of only text, ChatGPT now builds answers out of streamable components like side-by-side comparisons, editable charts, forms, maps, and small tools (a bill splitter, a savings calculator) that render while the model is still generating. OpenAI also says GPT-6 starts answering web-search questions about 44% sooner than GPT-5.6 Instant and makes better calls about when to search. The release targets ChatGPT's 1.2 billion-plus weekly users; users can dial the visuals down, and Enterprise access depends on admin settings.

Why it matters. The world's most-used AI assistant just started turning product questions into on-the-spot comparison tables and calculators. If ChatGPT is where buyers research, your structured product data — specs, pricing, reviews — is now raw material for interfaces you don't control. That's the same answer-engine visibility work showing up in the AI-marketing job postings below.

Research & Model Releases

Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5 at ~75% lower cost than Haiku 4.5

Anthropic's new small model costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100K — matching OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna on input price — and is the first Haiku with an adjustable "effort" setting. Anthropic's own benchmarks put it at 72.4% on OSWorld 2.1 (computer use), versus 15.7% for Haiku 4.5. HubSpot reported 92.8% on its CRM simulation suite, the best score it has seen there; Asana reported 30%+ lower task latency. Anthropic also halved Sonnet 5.5 cache-read pricing and added monthly API credits for Max and Team subscribers.

Why it matters. Cheap, fast models are what make "agent runs on every lead record" affordable. A HubSpot CRM benchmark in a launch post is a signal of where these small models are headed: routine CRM hygiene, enrichment, and routing.

Perplexity open-sources embedding models that search text, images, and PDF pages together

Perplexity released pplx-embed-v2-late, two "late-interaction" retrieval models that index text, images, and rendered document pages in one shared space: a 0.6B model for cheap, on-device retrieval and a 9B model that reports 92.4% on the MADQA document-QA benchmark. Weights are on Hugging Face.

Why it matters. Most content libraries are PDFs, decks, and images, not clean text. Models like this make "search our whole asset library" tools more accurate — and more buildable in-house.

Research Papers

MiniCorp: The Last Mile of the AI Agent Firm

Microsoft researchers built a simulator in which AI agents run an e-commerce company: an outer layer models customers, competitors, and market rules, while agents inside review events, discuss options, and make decisions that change the market. Checkpoints let the same situation be replayed with different choices — something historical business data can't offer. The agents coordinated across roles and adapted to market feedback, and when given explicit long-term strategy, they kept investing in advertising even when early returns were weak. The abstract reports no headline metrics.

Why it matters. The "do we cut this channel after a slow first month?" judgment is exactly what labs are now trying to teach agents. Expect agent tools that propose budget decisions to be tested in simulated markets like this before they touch your real ones.

Regulatory & Policy Watch

Connecticut's new AI and privacy laws start phasing in

Parts of Connecticut's AI law (Public Act 26-15) and a companion privacy law (Public Act 26-64) were scheduled to take effect Oct. 1: AI-subscription disclosures, whistleblower protections for people working on frontier models, a state system to track and regulate data brokers, a ban on selling residents' genetic data, and restrictions on facial recognition and surveillance pricing. Chatbots must disclose they aren't human starting Jan. 1, 2027, and companies need a framework disclosing AI use in employment decisions by Oct. 1, 2027. The attorney general handles most enforcement.

Why it matters for marketing-ops. If you buy third-party data, check whether your brokers are registering under the new system. And if you run dynamic or personalized pricing, the surveillance-pricing limits are worth a read with counsel.
PolicySep 28, 2026·CT Mirror

Australia moves toward mandatory "prove it's safe" rules for frontier AI

Assistant Minister Andrew Charlton said the government is finalising national standards for frontier AI by the end of 2026, with legislation planned for 2027, requiring developers to show they continually test for risks and prevent harm. The approach borrows from banking and aviation supervision rather than banning specific behaviours. Charlton argued voluntary safeguards aren't enough under competitive pressure.

Why it matters for marketing-ops. This targets model developers, not their customers. But "show your testing" regimes tend to flow down to vendor contracts, so expect more AI-vendor questionnaires about testing and incident reporting.

AI at Work & Marketing

ICANN reveals 1,615 new domain bids — and ".agent" is the hottest AI string

ICANN published 1,615 applications from 481 applicants in its new top-level-domain round. ".agent" drew 13 bids, including OpenAI, Meta, and Google — more than .agi (7) and .asi (5) combined. OpenAI applied for 15 strings (.chatgpt, .codex, .gpt, .mcp and more), Meta for 18, Google's registry for 39, and Anthropic for two (.anthropic, .claude). Applicants can swap strings through Oct. 21, and nothing is approved yet.

Why it matters. The AI labs are buying brand real estate for an agent-to-agent web. If ".agent" addresses become how AI agents find businesses, branded domains could join SEO and answer-engine visibility on the marketing-ops checklist.

An AI agent that files your Google Ads refund claims

Ad-fraud researcher FouAnalytics and fraud0 are bringing fraud0's agent to large advertisers. It monitors traffic, builds the evidence, and runs Google Ads refund claims end to end for invalid traffic, false clicks, and billing errors, then blocks recurring bad placements. Setup is a JavaScript tag plus a connected Ads account. One customer, Uriach, recovered $28,000 in three months.

Why it matters. This is a clean, low-risk agent use case for paid-media ops: tedious, evidence-driven, and easy to measure in dollars recovered.
