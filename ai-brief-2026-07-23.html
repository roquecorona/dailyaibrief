Agent Guardrails
The policies, escalation rules, and approval boundaries that define what an autonomous AI agent is allowed to do on its own versus when it has to hand off to a person. OpenAI built its entire enterprise pitch for Presence (today's top marketing-relevant launch) around this — for marketing-ops teams evaluating AI agents for CX or campaign execution, "what are the guardrails" is now the first vendor question, not an afterthought.

Earnings & Infrastructure  ·  Jul 22, 2026

Alphabet's Q2 shows the AI trade's split personality — 82% cloud growth, then a 5% stock drop

Alphabet's Q2 2026 revenue hit $119.8B, up 24% year-over-year and ahead of estimates, with Google Cloud revenue jumping 82% to $24.8B on enterprise AI Solutions and Infrastructure demand — nearly 90% of the Fortune 100 now use Gemini Enterprise, and Gemini models process 22 billion API tokens per minute. Despite the beat, GOOGL fell almost 5% after-hours once Alphabet raised its full-year 2026 capex guidance to $195B–$205B (up from $180B–$190B), with negative free cash flow of -$5.9B for the quarter.

Why it matters. This is the clearest real-time read yet on the tension defining the AI trade: enterprise AI demand is showing up in real cloud revenue, not just pilot programs, but investors are increasingly nervous about what it costs to keep serving that demand. If your org is budgeting for AI tools, it's the same conversation happening one level up — has the ROI shown up yet, or are we still funding the buildout.

Regulatory & Policy Watch

Update: EU's Article 50 disclosure deadline is 10 days out — and content-marking gets a longer runway

As covered here previously, chatbot-disclosure and deepfake-labeling duties still land on schedule August 2, 2026. New detail: per the European Council–Parliament's May 7 agreement, the specific obligation to mark AI-generated content from systems already on the market before Aug 2 gets a grace period extending to December 2, 2026. Penalties for non-compliance top out at €15M or 3% of global turnover.

Why it matters. If you're staffing up EU disclosure banners for Aug 2, the chatbot and deepfake rules still apply on time — but you get a longer runway specifically on watermarking pre-existing AI content.
PolicyEffective Aug 2, 2026·Lexology · EU AI Act

Update: FTC's AI-accuracy comment window closes July 31 — final stretch to weigh in

The FTC's proposed policy statement — arguing that hiding how an AI system's outputs are steered toward undisclosed objectives can violate Section 5, and that it would preempt conflicting state rules like Colorado's AI Act — has a public comment period closing July 31, 2026.

Why it matters. If your martech stack uses any AI system tuned toward specific outcomes — recommendation ranking, ad targeting, brand-safety filtering — the disclosure safe-harbor standard being finalized here could set the bar for what counts as adequate transparency.

AI at Work & Marketing

OpenAI launches Presence, an enterprise platform for "trusted" AI voice and chat agents

Presence bundles model reasoning with policies, guardrails, and escalation rules so companies define exactly what an agent can do before a human has to step in. It already powers OpenAI's own English-language phone support line, resolving 75% of inbound issues without a person; launch customers include BBVA Mexico, SoftBank, and Retail Insurance Australia. It's limited-GA for enterprise customers only, not self-serve yet.
