Claude Fable 5's included access closes tonight — usage credits start tomorrow

Anthropic's third extension of Fable 5's plan-included access lapses tonight at 11:59 PM PT. Starting July 20, Pro and Team Standard subscribers shift to prepaid usage credits — $10 per million input tokens, $50 per million output (the priciest per-token rate Anthropic lists; the Batch API halves it, and cached input drops to $1/M) — with a one-time $100 credit to start. Max and Team Premium subscribers keep Fable 5 in-plan for up to 50% of weekly limits. Three pushed deadlines in five weeks, plus a mysterious "Claude Honeycomb" listing that briefly surfaced in Cursor this week, have kept speculation alive that Opus 5 is what Anthropic is really waiting on.

Why it matters. For anyone metering Claude spend on a marketing-ops budget, tonight's cutover is the moment "included" frontier access starts showing up as a line item — worth flagging before Monday's usage reports land.

Industry & Business

How AI could create a fiscal crisis

Bloomberg's weekend Forecast newsletter lays out the macro risk behind the AI buildout: advanced-economy government debt has climbed from about 70% of GDP in 2007 to 110% in 2025, and the U.S. federal deficit is on pace for $2 trillion this year. The five largest hyperscalers have added roughly $350 billion in debt over five years to fund AI infrastructure, and global AI-related debt issuance is projected to hit $570 billion in 2026. AI could improve the budget picture over time, but Bloomberg's modeling suggests offsetting costs could erase half to two-thirds of any deficit benefit — and bond investors are already showing softer demand for hyperscaler debt.

Why it matters. The AI capex boom is increasingly a debt story, not just a compute story — a useful macro frame for anyone justifying AI budget asks in a tightening environment.
