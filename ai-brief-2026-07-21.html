Infrastructure  ·  Jul 20, 2026

AMD ships Helios, its first rack-scale AI system — and Microsoft signs on

AMD delivered its first Helios rack-scale AI system on July 20, pairing 72 Instinct MI455X GPUs with 6th-gen EPYC "Venice" CPUs and Pensando networking in a liquid-cooled design built to go head-to-head with Nvidia's Grace Blackwell and Vera Rubin platforms. Microsoft joined as a new buyer alongside Meta, OpenAI, and Oracle — Meta alone has committed to up to 6 gigawatts of AMD GPUs over time, starting with 1 gigawatt on Helios racks later this year.

Why it matters. This is the first commercially credible rival to Nvidia's near-monopoly on AI compute, arriving the same week cheap open-weight models are already pressuring chip valuations. Cheaper, more competitive compute is the upstream cost that eventually shows up in what you pay for AI seats in your martech stack.

Regulatory & Policy Watch

FTC seeks comment on a policy statement targeting "suppression of accuracy" in AI

The FTC's proposed statement says steering a model's output away from an accurate answer — for ideological reasons, political pressure, or to dodge state-law liability — without disclosure can amount to deception under Section 5. Public comment runs through July 31, 2026.

Why it matters. If AI copy or recommendation tools in your stack are tuned toward specific outcomes without saying so, this proposal treats that as a potential deceptive-practice issue. Worth a compliance read before your next AI-content audit — see today's Term of the Day.
PolicyComment period: Jul 1–31, 2026·FTC.gov

US weighs a FINRA-style watchdog to vet frontier AI models

A proposal reportedly developed with Treasury input would create an independent AI agency, reporting to the SEC and modeled on FINRA, to screen frontier models for dangerous capabilities before release. It's under White House review; nothing is finalized.

Why it matters. A formal model-certification body, if it happens, adds a new gate above the AI vendors marketing teams buy from — plan for longer procurement and compliance cycles when evaluating new AI tools.

AI at Work & Marketing

Google gives original recipe-site links more prominence in AI Mode

After sustained publisher complaints that AI-generated answers reproduce a page's value without sending traffic back, Google is placing direct links to original recipe pages more prominently in AI Mode responses — a narrow test case for a much bigger question.

Why it matters. The same attribution fight applies to any content you publish that AI Overviews might summarize. Watch how Google handles the recipe case as a preview of what's coming for other content categories, including yours.
