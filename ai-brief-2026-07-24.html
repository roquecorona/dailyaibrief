Term of the Day
Model Distillation
Training a smaller, cheaper model to mimic a larger one by learning from its outputs. It's a legitimate, widely-used efficiency technique in small doses — but at industrial, covert scale (what the White House is now alleging Moonshot AI did to Anthropic's Fable to build Kimi K3) it's treated as unauthorized IP extraction. Worth knowing before you take a vendor's "efficient AI model" claim at face value.

By the Numbers
Google's new ATLAS report (15M Gemini interactions across 150 countries) finds AI touches 68% of occupations — but reaches a median of just 21% of tasks within them, and fewer than 10% of non-routine cognitive conversations aim for full end-to-end automation. On-the-ground AI use still looks like collaboration, not replacement.

Model Espionage  ·  Jul 23, 2026

White House accuses Moonshot AI of covertly distilling Anthropic's Fable to build Kimi K3

Michael Kratsios, director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, said Moonshot AI built a purpose-made internal system to run large-scale distillation against Anthropic's Fable model, rotating access routes to avoid detection — and separately alleged Moonshot accessed banned Nvidia GB300 chips routed through Thailand. It's the first time a senior US official has directly named a specific Chinese lab for copying a specific American model. Independent researchers are split: Redwood Research found Kimi K3 identifies as Claude disproportionately often, but other experts told TechCrunch distillation alone doesn't explain how good K3 actually is. No penalties have been announced; Moonshot hasn't responded, and full K3 weights are still due out by July 27.

Why it matters. This is the sharpest US-China flashpoint yet over open-weight models — and it's landing right as marketing and ops teams are quietly adopting cheaper Chinese open models in their own stacks.

Regulatory & Policy Watch

State AI-deepfake election laws face their first real test this cycle

Thirty-one states now require disclosure of AI-generated content in campaign materials, but enforcement is proving spotty — unlabeled AI ads have already surfaced in Michigan, Oregon, and Kentucky races, and courts have struck down deepfake laws in California and Hawaii on free-speech grounds (though California's separate labeling law survives).

Why it matters for marketing-ops. Any brand or agency work touching election-adjacent or persona-style synthetic content needs a state-by-state disclosure check — labeling rules, satire exemptions, and enforcement all vary, and "AI-generated" labels don't automatically satisfy every state's law.

AI at Work & Marketing

Google's first ATLAS report: AI is a collaborator, not (yet) a replacement

Analyzing 14.65M de-identified interactions across Gemini, AI Mode, and the Gemini API, Google mapped work-related activity to 4,000 tasks across 800 occupations in 150 countries. People mostly use AI as a partner — for drafting, research, and troubleshooting — not to delegate whole tasks; over 86% of all AI interactions in the study happen outside of work entirely.
